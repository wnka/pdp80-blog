For me and my brain, this game is absolutely perfect. 10/10. It's now one of my favorite games of all time.

I started playing it shortly after the new remastered version came out (Sept 2025) but quickly hit a wall and gave up, mostly because I didn't understand how to play and grow your characters. I just thought "damn this game's hard" but after all my other JRPGing I came back to it and learned to love it.

With shmups I always loved how when you started playing a game it always felt like getting a 1cc was impossible, but little by little you could get better until finally you could piece together a run and get a 1cc. I got that same feeling with FFT because there are battles that feel completely impossible when you first play them since you've stacked your abilities totally wrong for the situation. The rooftop battle at the end of Chapter 3 is a good example of this. The first time I played it I think I was able to make one move, and not even attack, and it was over. It was a real "WTF this is impossible" moment, but then when I figured it out it felt great.

There's some grinding when you realize you need to get enough to get good at some job you've never used before. Some jobs on surface look stupid and pointless but then there's a wonderful "aha" moment where you see where their strength. The grinding never felt tedious because there's always enough variety in the encounters and you're kind of figuring out what this new job you've never used before is good at and how it complements your other abilities.

I went though the whole game without looking at guides or hints on the internet. Now that I beat it, I want to go research and learn what others do, what the right team composition is, etc etc. [There was a speedrun at AGDQ 2026 earlier this year](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ALWCf7GJQbU) which shows some crazy techniques, like attacking yourself and other things you'd never think is desirable.

And of course I've gotta also mention that my daughter enjoyed the game too. It was funny for her to come up to me and say "Dad can we play Final Fantasy Tactics?" and be into it. She made plans, drew maps, and I let her play quite a bit. She was able to win battles pretty regularly!

And the music! The music is so good. Many songs were done by Hitoshi Sakimoto, who in an overlap with my shmupping also did the fantastic music for Radiant Silvergun (love the music of that game, don't love the game so much, not my thing). His songs here sound super similar to the Silvergun soundtrack, you could probably swap songs and they'd still fit.

I don't know what I want to play next, they actually just released v1.5 of this which adds "New Game+" which I started messing around with. But definitely another Tactical RPG. Fire Emblem? Triangle Strategy? We shall see!
