For me and my brain, this game is absolutely perfect. 10/10.

I just finished it and it was amazing. I played the new re-release "The Ivalice Chronicles" on Steam and this is now easily one of my favorite games.

I started playing it shortly after it came out (Sept 2025) but quickly hit a wall and gave up, mostly because I didn't understand how to play and grow your characters. I just thought "damn this game's hard" but after all my other JRPGing I came back to it.

I want to play some more "Tactical RPGs" now because if they're like this, I'm so in. 

One of the things I loved about grinding to 1cc a shmup was that at the beginning it always felt impossible. But little by little you got better and better and finally you could piece together a run and get a 1cc. I got that same kind of feeling with FFT because there are battles that feel completely impossible when you first play them, because the way you've stacked your abilities becomes totally wrong for the situation. The rooftop battle at the end of Chapter 3 is a good example of this. The first time I played it I think I was able to make one move, and not even attack, and it was over. It was a real "WTF this is impossible" moment, but then using the power of my brain I was able to figure it out.

There's some grinding in these situations, because you realize you need to get enough to get good at some job you've never used before and that one the surface looks stupid and pointless but then there's a wonderful "aha" moment where you stop thinking those skills are pointless and realize they're amazing. The grinding never felt tedious though, because there's always enough variety in the encounters and you're kind of figuring out what this new job you've never used before is good at and how it complements your other abilities.

The other RPGs I've played have that element of exploration, walking around on a world map and spending some time trying to figure out where you're supposed to go. But here you don't explore. You know where you need to go and battle, you just can't win initially because your shits all out of whack. So you gotta go do some baby battles to gain the right skills.

I went though the whole game without looking at guides or hints on the internet. Now that I beat it, I want to go research and learn what others do, what the right team composition is, etc etc. I'm sure there are crazy speedruns of the game too. [There was actually one at SGDQ 2026 earlier this month!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ALWCf7GJQbU). 2 hours!? According to Steam this took me about 80 hours to beat.

Gotta also mention that my daughter enjoyed the game too, it was funny for her to come up to me and say "Dad can we play Final Fantasy Tactics?" and be into it. She made plans, drew maps, and I let her play quite a bit. She was able to win battles pretty regularly! So fun.

I don't know what I want to play next, they actually just released v1.5 of this which adds "New Game+" which I might mess around with. But definitely another Tactical RPG. Fire Emblem? Triangle Strategy? We shall see.

And the music! The music is so good. Many songs were done by Hitoshi Sakimoto, which in an overlap with my shmup days did the fantastic music for Radiant Silvergun (love the music of that game, don't love the game so much, not my thing). His songs here sound super similar to the Silvergun soundtrack, you could probably swap songs and they'd still fit.

I also played all the way through the original Quake 1 due to it's [recent 30th anniversary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y3Wekr-wnic), that game holds up! I played a ton of that on my Pentium/60.
